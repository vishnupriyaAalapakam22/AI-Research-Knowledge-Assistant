Title: Applications of Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being used in healthcare.
Machine learning can help doctors analyze medical images and identify
patterns in patient data.

One important application is disease prediction. AI models can analyze
large amounts of patient information and identify potential health risks.

However, AI systems have limitations. They can produce incorrect
predictions when the training data is incomplete or biased.

Conclusion:
Artificial Intelligence can support healthcare professionals, but
human expertise remains important.
